PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT
	EN	Mixcloud Direct

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_MENU
	EN	Mixcloud

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_DESC
	EN	Mixcloud for Lyrion servers that can't run the official plugin. Built for Lyrion 9.0 servers that can't be upgraded, and tested on 8.5, 9.0, 9.1 and 9.2; no helper application needed. Read-only: browse, search and play, and see your public profile by username. Manage your Mixcloud account on mixcloud.com.

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_ABOUT
	EN	Built for Lyrion servers that can't be upgraded to 9.2, where the official Mixcloud plugin can neither log in nor play. This plugin plays Mixcloud's own streams directly, with no yt-dlp or other helper, so it runs on older servers and modest hardware.

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_READ_ONLY_TEXT
	EN	This plugin never logs in to Mixcloud and can't change your account. It reads your public profile by username. To follow, favourite, upload or change anything on Mixcloud, use mixcloud.com or the Mixcloud app; changes show up here within about 10 minutes. "Follow in Lyrion" is separate: it's kept on this Lyrion server only and isn't sent to Mixcloud.

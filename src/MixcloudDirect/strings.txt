PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT
	EN	Mixcloud Direct

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_MENU
	EN	Mixcloud

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_DESC
	EN	Browse and play Mixcloud shows: search, categories, and any user's uploads, favourites, playlists and follows. Plays the 192k stream with no helper application, and works on Lyrion 8 and 9.

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_ABOUT
	EN	Plays Mixcloud's own streams directly (no yt-dlp or other helper), so it runs on any Lyrion server. Instead of logging in, it reads your public profile by username: uploads, favourites, listening history, playlists and the people you follow.

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_VERSION
	EN	Version

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_USERNAME
	EN	Mixcloud username

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_USERNAME_DESC
	EN	Your Mixcloud username (the part after mixcloud.com/ in your profile link) or the profile link itself. It fills "My Mixcloud". No password needed: this reads your public profile.

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_QUALITY
	EN	Stream quality

PLUGIN_MIXCLOUDDIRECT_QUALITY_DESC
